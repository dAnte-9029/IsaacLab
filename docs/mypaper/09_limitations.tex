\section{Limitations and Sensitivity}
This paper uses a quasi-steady strip-theory aerodynamic model to evaluate duty-cycle modulation tradeoffs.
While this model is computationally efficient and suitable for extensive parameter sweeps, it may not capture unsteady vortex dynamics, wake capture, and complex membrane-wing aeroelastic effects.
In particular, separation modeling is highly sensitive to stall thresholds and dynamic-stall parameters, which can qualitatively affect trim existence and mean thrust predictions.
Therefore, the main quantitative results in this paper are reported with the separated-flow model disabled to provide a controlled baseline; separated-flow effects are treated as a sensitivity and a limitation.
