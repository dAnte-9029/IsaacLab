## 2. Implementation

`scripts/flapping_rl/find_periodic_trim.py` performs a batched fixed-root search.
It settles the native frequency dynamics, measures an integer number of
unwrapped mechanism cycles and averages the environment's total aerodynamic
force and moment diagnostics. Aerodynamic moment is transferred from base COM
to the instantaneous whole-articulation system COM before scoring. Gravity is
added to force in the body FLU frame.

The workflow supports two score modes:

- `longitudinal`: norm of Fx/Fz plus absolute My;
- `full_wrench`: norms of all three force and all three moment residuals.

Candidate tables are written before the free-flight release so a release-stage
failure cannot erase the fixed-root evidence. Native startup failures exit
nonzero even though Isaac shutdown can otherwise mask a pending Python error.

## 3. Executed search sequence

1. An eight-candidate integration gate verified artifact generation and exposed
   an invalid 179 degree termination override. The implemented tilt predicate
   uses `sin(threshold)`, so 179 degrees behaves like approximately 1 degree.
   The diagnostic workflow now uses exactly 90 degrees to disable only the
   automatic tilt reset during open-loop recording.
2. A 125-candidate coarse longitudinal grid found no passing candidate. Its
   best point lay on the 12 degree, 3 Hz and -6 degree elevon boundaries.
3. A 252-candidate expanded longitudinal grid produced eight preliminary
   longitudinal passes. Its best point was 12 degrees, 2.75 Hz and -8 degrees.
4. A 25-candidate lateral sweep showed that rudder should remain near zero and
   approximately -3 degrees differential elevon cancels the mean roll moment.
5. A 225-candidate joint full-wrench refinement produced two strict passes. Its
   best grid point was 11.75 degrees pitch, 2.625 Hz, -7 degrees symmetric
   elevon, zero rudder and -3 degrees differential elevon.
6. A local finite-difference interpolation was independently remeasured for 16
   mechanism cycles in a fresh process.

## 5. Free-flight boundary

The same fixed action did not remain near the fixed-root condition after
release. Within the first 0.25 s, nose-up pitch changed from approximately
11.7 degrees to -9.1 degrees. Over three seconds, height decreased by about 4.9
m and roll reached approximately -72 degrees.

This does not invalidate the reported mean-wrench calculation. It shows that
mean wrench balance alone is not sufficient to initialize the measured
multibody system on a free-flight periodic orbit. The fixed-root operation
suppresses phase-dependent base motion and momentum exchange generated by the
moving measured wings. A true periodic trim requires a phase-conditioned
Poincare or multiple-shooting solve for the periodic root velocity, angular
velocity and attitude together with the fixed action.

The candidate therefore must not yet replace the PureRL reset defaults. It is a
high-quality fixed-root wrench-balance seed for the periodic-orbit solve and a
reference point for four-channel action-authority tests.
