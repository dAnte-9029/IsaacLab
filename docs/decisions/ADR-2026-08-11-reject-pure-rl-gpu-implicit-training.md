# ADR-2026-08-11: Reject GPU implicit PureRL training route

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-11
- Scope: PureRL training backend selection
- Supersedes: `ADR-2026-08-11-pure-rl-gpu-implicit-zero-shot-qualification.md`

## Context

The direct-GPU implicit candidate was investigated as a faster training plant for policies that would later be
promoted on the authoritative CPU-native holonomic plant. Phase matching reduced fixed-root wing tracking error,
and the isolated tail-servo comparison passed. The frozen free-flight paired-plant gate nevertheless failed when
GPU replayed the exact 60 Hz actions generated by CPU-native.

Across the selected C1 and C2a cases, the candidate exceeded frozen limits for moment mismatch, one-second
position divergence, or one-second attitude divergence. The remaining discrepancy appeared within the first
wingbeat despite close wing and tail angles, consistent with unequal root-reaction dynamics between the native
constraint and the implicit drive plus mimic relation. The result is recorded in
`docs/audits/2026-08-11-pure-rl-gpu-implicit-paired-plant-gate.md`.

## Decision

Reject the direct-GPU implicit plant as a PureRL curriculum training or policy-transfer route. Stop further
gain, phase, tail, reward, or paired-plant tuning for this route. Resume C1 retention and C2a/C2b/C2c curriculum
training on the CPU-native plant at 480 Hz physics and 60 Hz policy rate.

The GPU candidate implementation and generated diagnostic contracts remain available for reproducibility and
must stay explicitly labelled screening-only. They are not defaults, promotion authorities, or acceptable
sources of curriculum checkpoints.

Training acceleration must preserve the CPU-native plant. Permitted follow-up lanes are host CPU configuration,
CPU environment-count scaling, separating rollout training from checkpoint evaluation, measured hotspot
optimization with regression gates, and an independently benchmarked policy-optimizer device change that does
not alter simulation physics.

## Alternatives considered

- Continue with a free-root reaction-wrench diagnostic: rejected because the observed backend difference already
  exceeds the policy-transfer gate and further attribution would not make the candidate an equivalent plant.
- Relax the paired thresholds: rejected because the thresholds were frozen before the comparison and represent
  the required trajectory-level fidelity.
- Use GPU implicit only for early curricula and switch later: rejected because early policies and representations
  would still adapt to the unequal root-reaction dynamics.
- Remove all GPU candidate code: rejected because retaining the explicit experimental path and negative evidence
  supports reproducibility without changing authoritative defaults.

## Consequences

- All new curriculum checkpoints are trained and promoted on CPU-native.
- Direct-GPU throughput is no longer part of the active PureRL acceleration plan.
- CPU-native performance work requires end-to-end PPO timing; fixed-root raw environment-step numbers alone are
  insufficient.
- The prior GPU qualification ADR remains as historical procedure but is superseded by this stop decision.

## Assumptions and validation

This decision assumes the paired schedule, checkpoint, action replay, reset state, and frozen thresholds in the
paired-plant audit are valid. It is supported by the passing drive and tail gates and the failing free-flight
paired gate. It does not claim that implicit drives are generally unusable; it rejects this candidate for the
current measured PureRL plant and curriculum transfer contract.

## Reconsideration

Reconsider only if a future GPU backend implements the native holonomic constraint or a new physical model is
validated independently against CPU-native free-flight reaction and policy-level gates. Parameter retuning of
the current implicit candidate alone is not a reconsideration trigger.
